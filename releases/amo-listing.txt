ENGLISH — SUMMARY
Pause before distracting websites. Write your intention, then confirm with a PIN or authenticator code. Choose your domains and browse more mindfully.

ENGLISH — DESCRIPTION
Opening a website out of habit? Wake Up Neo gives you a moment to decide why you want to go there.

Before visiting a selected domain, answer a short question in at least five words and confirm with a PIN on a shuffled keypad or a six-digit code from your authenticator app. You can also close the tab instead.

- Choose the domains you want to pause before visiting, including their subdomains.
- Customize the question, or disable it and keep PIN or TOTP verification.
- Switch between English and Slovak.
- Keep your settings on your device. Your answers are not saved or sent to a server.

No account is required. To get started, open the extension settings and choose your domains and verification method. TOTP requires an external authenticator app. This is a tool for mindful browsing, not a security or parental-control system.

SLOVENČINA — SÚHRN (Summary)

SLOVENČINA — POPIS (Description)
Otváraš Facebook, YouTube či iný web zo zvyku? Wake Up Neo ti dá chvíľu na zamyslenie, prečo tam chceš ísť.

Pred návštevou vybranej domény napíšeš odpoveď na krátku otázku aspoň piatimi slovami. Pokračovanie potvrdíš PIN-om na klávesnici, ktorej číslice sa pri každej návšteve premiešajú, alebo šesťmiestnym kódom z overovacej aplikácie. Ak sa rozhodneš nepokračovať, môžeš panel zavrieť.

- Vyber si weby, pred ktorými sa chceš zastaviť. Pravidlo platí aj pre ich subdomény.
- Nastav si vlastnú otázku alebo povinnú odpoveď vypni a používaj iba overenie PIN-om či TOTP kódom.
- Používaj rozšírenie v slovenčine alebo angličtine.
- Nastavenia zostávajú na tvojom zariadení. Tvoje odpovede sa neukladajú ani neposielajú na server.

Účet nepotrebuješ. Na začiatok otvor nastavenia rozšírenia a vyber domény a spôsob overenia. TOTP vyžaduje externú overovaciu aplikáciu. Ide o pomôcku na vedomé prehliadanie, nie o bezpečnostný nástroj alebo rodičovskú kontrolu.

NOTES FOR REVIEWERS — SKOPÍRUJ DO SAMOSTATNÉHO POĽA
Changes in version 0.2.0:
- Added Chromium support in the shared codebase, using webextension-polyfill 0.12.0 and separate browser manifests. The AMO package contains only the Firefox build (background.scripts); the Chromium build uses background.service_worker and is distributed separately.
- Replaced webRequest/webRequestBlocking navigation interception with declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess redirect rules for main-frame requests to configured domains. webNavigation remains in use for navigation handling, and storage is used for local settings. Additional domain access is requested through optional host permissions when configuring domains.
- Refactored shared form controls and settings reads, refined layout and design tokens, and unified branding around one PNG icon.

Build and testing instructions are included in README.md in the source archive. Use Node.js 24.19.0 and npm 11.17.0, then run npm ci --include=dev and npm run build. The Firefox build is generated in dist/firefox/; compare that directory with the submitted Firefox ZIP. npm run build also generates a separate Chromium build in dist/chromium/, which is not part of the AMO extension package. The polyfill dependency version and registry source are recorded in package-lock.json, and its license is bundled in the extension.

Validation warnings: The two "Unsafe assignment to innerHTML" warnings in assets/main-pSVCpxJe.js (line 9, columns 1781 and 4696) originate from the bundled React DOM 19.3.1 production renderer, specifically its internal handling of dangerouslySetInnerHTML. The extension's application source does not use innerHTML or dangerouslySetInnerHTML. User-provided values are rendered as React text children or form field values, not injected as HTML. React DOM's exact dependency version and registry source are recorded in package-lock.json.

No account is required. The default verification method is PIN (initial PIN: 1234); it can be changed in settings. To test, visit facebook.com, enter an answer of at least five words and confirm with the initial PIN, or close the tab. TOTP is also available and requires an external authenticator app. Processing and storage happen locally; the extension does not transmit user data.
